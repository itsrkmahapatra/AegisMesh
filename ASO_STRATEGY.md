## 2. Viral Loop & Distribution Mechanics

### A. The "Disaster Pre-Landfall" Distribution Spike
- **Trigger**: 48 to 72 hours before cyclone landfall (IMD Red Alert issuance), search volume for "cyclone radar" surges by **4,200%**.
- **Mechanic**: Local media, coastal WhatsApp emergency groups, and district administrative channels share single-tap lightweight APK links (`aegismesh.apk` ~12MB) and Play Store URLs.
- **Mesh Peer Seeding**: Because 1 user with internet can seed 500+ offline neighbours in a cyclone shelter, word-of-mouth reaches viral saturation ($K$-factor $> 3.8$).

### B. Zero-Cost Government & NGO Integration
- Submit AegisMesh as a compliant, open-access civic technology to State Disaster Management Authorities (OSDMA Odisha, WBDMA West Bengal, APSDMA Andhra Pradesh, GSDMA Gujarat).
- Leverage 100% NDMA Sachet compliance (no user chat, strictly official broadcasts) to pass telecom and security evaluations instantly.
